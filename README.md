This document tracks the live review of the candidate pool for the Winter 2026 gift
bundle programme. The supplier catalog under `catalog/` is the machine-readable
counterpart of this file; keep the two in sync whenever the candidate pool changes.

## Campaign Brief

The Winter Gift Bundle ships to retail partners in three waves. Each bundle pairs a
single title with a hand-made reading accessory. Buying decisions are made per title,
not per supplier, and the team only sources from suppliers whose status is current.
